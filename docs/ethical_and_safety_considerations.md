# Ethical and safety considerations

Traffic-Qwen V1 is an offline research experiment. It is not tested on public roads, certified, or designed to connect to real traffic infrastructure. Simulation abstractions, source-data assumptions, and unmeasured emergency conditions can create dangerous errors if transferred directly to real signals.

Do not deploy this model on public infrastructure. A future field study would require qualified traffic engineers, a validated safety case, independent testing, fail-safe hardware, regulatory review, and a human-controlled rollback path. No personal or vehicle-identifying data should be published with traces.
