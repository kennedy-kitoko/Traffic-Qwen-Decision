# Future work

1. Obtain legally redistributable or explicitly authorized multi-episode traffic data.
2. Generate V2 labels from verified CityFlow counterfactual branches with a shared continuation policy and documented reward components.
3. Balance phase-duration coverage using genuine additional trajectories, not fabricated labels.
4. Evaluate several seeds, held-out episodes, congestion profiles, and another city.
5. Measure movement-level starvation and spillback from simulator state.
6. Revisit calibration on a separate validation set and determine whether calibrated probabilities should affect decisions.
7. Compare against MaxPressure and JevLight with repeated identical scenarios and report uncertainty.
8. Treat real-world deployment as a separate safety-critical research program.
